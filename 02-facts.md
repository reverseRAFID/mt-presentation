## ✅ Hypersonic (2025 rover) — for the story, not the spec slide

- 48 kg · rocker-bogie with arched base support · 6-DOF arm
- CNC-cut aerospace-grade aluminium chassis, optimised in SolidWorks and Ansys, field-tested across rural Bangladesh
- **6-DOF arm inverse kinematics built with recycled brushed DC motors for under BDT 20,000, against a typical commercial cost of BDT 5,00,000+** (Daily Star)
- **Jamdani-inspired motifs etched into the CNC-cut aluminium shell**; wheel illustrations carry symbols from Bangladesh's culture and revolutionary history (Daily Star)

## ⚠️ Conflicts inside your own materials

These matter because the deck will be seen by people who can check. Resolve before the build.

1. **"11th place, best result to date" is wrong on your public site.** Both the homepage ("#11 Best world rank — URC 2024") and the About timeline ("2024 — URC 11th place — best result to date") say this, while your own competitions page, your achievements page, and the VC deck all record **21st in 2024**. The 11th appears to be the **2019** result, misfiled. Your achievements page separately says best global rank is **#7**, directly contradicting the homepage on the same site.

2. **Founding year is stated four different ways.** Site: founded 2017. Homepage: "10+ years active" and "run on people who decided to help since 2013." PCBWay: "started our journey in 2015." VC deck: "past 8 years." Daily Star: "seven years at the URC." Pick one and use it everywhere.

3. **URC debut year.** The About timeline says "2022 — Debut at URC." BRACU's own 2018 press release and the VC deck both show a URC 2018 entry finishing 13th. The timeline is wrong.

4. **The old site (`bracu-mongoltori.com`) is stale and contradicts everything else** — it claims 21st place with 210.38 points in URC **2025** (the correct 2025 result is 8th with 317.87), and a 4th place in URC 2021 (finals cancelled). The Mars Society still links to this domain from its team list. Worth fixing separately; do not cite it.

5. **Team size:** homepage says both "70+ Team Members" and "60+ Active members." The deck uses ~60.

6. **Rover count:** homepage says "7+ rovers built"; the rovers page lists 8. The deck uses eight.

7. **2020 and 2021 need careful phrasing.** URC finals were cancelled both years. "3rd place at URC 2020" without qualification is not defensible; "3rd on the design review submission" is.
